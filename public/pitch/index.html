Built on AI · 01 of 03

AI-Powered Real Estate Analysis

Paste any UK portal link, Rightmove, Zoopla, OnTheMarket or PrimeLocation, and Buynidify's AI reads the listing and returns a full investment breakdown in seconds. No spreadsheets, no waiting on a broker, no manual data entry. Here is everything it actually gives an investor:

  • Gross rental yield for this exact property, benchmarked against the real local market average for that area
  • A rental-return chart plotting this property against comparable properties nearby, so "good yield" has real context
  • An interactive investment calculator: edit expected rent, running costs and vacancy assumptions and watch gross and net yield recalculate live
  • A full purchase-cost breakdown using real, current UK Stamp Duty Land Tax bands from gov.uk, itemised band by band, not a rough guess
  • Location and tenant-demand scoring, so an investor can see genuine appetite for that address, not just its price
  • Every figure sourced and labelled (Buynidify market research, gov.uk), never a black-box number with no explanation
  • Built-in disclaimers: clearly marked as an estimate, not financial or legal advice, with England/NI SDLT shown separately from Scotland and Wales rates

Built on AI · 02 of 03

Relocate AI

Linda, Buynidify's relocation concierge, walks international movers through an entire relocation in one guided conversation, from the first question to a signed tenancy:

  • A guided flow covering destination city, budget and reason for moving: work, study, family, EU settlement, or another route
  • Personalised visa and immigration guidance for that specific route, including exactly which documents are needed (passport, sponsorship or CAS, proof of funds and more, per route)
  • A parallel rental documents checklist too: photo ID, proof of income, references, and the right-to-rent check Buynidify handles once matched
  • A curated shortlist of real homes, each given an AI fit score against everything already answered, not just filtered by price
  • A local settling-in guide: opening a UK bank account, applying for a National Insurance number, registering with a GP, getting a SIM and broadband sorted
  • A simulated live translated voice call demo: Linda joins a call with a landlord, translating in real time, consent requested up front, with a bilingual summary and next steps at the end
  • Free-form chat for anything else, available any time, in the mover's own words

24/7 AI Support & Full Property Management

Emil is Buynidify's always-on support agent, and coordinates full property management on the investor's behalf, not just tenant-issue triage:

  • Instant replies, 24 hours a day, on every account, no waiting for office hours
  • Structured triage across maintenance, payments, lease questions and moving, so nothing is a vague "something's wrong" ticket
  • Urgency detection: emergencies like no heating, no hot water or an active leak are automatically fast-tracked to a local repair partner the same day
  • Full property management coordination: compliance and day-to-day upkeep, not just answering a question and closing the ticket
  • Every issue logged and tracked through to resolution, visible to the investor at every stage, nothing lost in an inbox or a missed call
  • All communication stays on the platform, verified and compliant, instead of scattering across phone calls, texts and emails
